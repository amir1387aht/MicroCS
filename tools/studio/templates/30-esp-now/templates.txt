[espnow.cs] ESP-NOW messages
Board-to-board packets without a router: add a peer, send, receive with a callback.

[espnow_broadcast.cs] Sensor beacons to every board
A sender broadcasts a JSON reading every 300 ms; receivers print who sent it and the signal
strength.

[espnow_secure.cs] Encrypted peer and remote LED
Pairs with one board using a 16-character key and toggles its LED on command; replies are
acknowledged.
