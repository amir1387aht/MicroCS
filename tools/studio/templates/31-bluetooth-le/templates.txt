# MicroCS Studio templates (Templates / Alt+T). One block per file, in the order Studio lists them:
#   [file] Display name   - a file in this folder
#   save as: name.cs      - optional: the name Studio suggests (default: the file name)
#   description lines     - shown in the list and the preview
category: Bluetooth LE
requires: Ble

[scanner.cs] BLE scanner
requires: Ble, Ble.Scan, StringExtra, Format
Lists nearby Bluetooth LE devices with name, signal strength, services and beacons.
Needs firmware built with BLE (docs/BLE.md); simulated with --sim.

[beacon.cs] iBeacon / Eddystone beacon
requires: Ble, Ble.Advertise
Advertises an iBeacon, then an Eddystone-URL, without accepting connections.

[heart_rate.cs] Heart-rate peripheral
requires: Ble, Ble.Server, Ble.Advertise
Standard heart-rate and battery services with notifications: fitness apps and nRF Connect
understand it.

[led_service.cs] LED control service
requires: GPIO, Ble, Ble.Server, Ble.Advertise
Your own 128-bit GATT service: a phone writes 01 / 00 to switch the LED and gets a counter
by notification.

[uart_echo.cs] BLE UART console
requires: Ble, BleUart
Nordic UART Service: chat with the board from a "Serial Bluetooth Terminal" app.

[central_client.cs] Central: read a sensor
requires: Ble, Ble.Scan, Ble.Central, Ble.Read, Ble.Subscribe
Finds a heart-rate sensor, connects, reads its battery level and subscribes to its readings.

[pairing.cs] Pairing with a passkey
requires: Ble, Ble.Server, Ble.Advertise, Ble.Security, Ble.Bonds
A characteristic that needs an authenticated link: the board shows a passkey, bonds are kept.

[diagnostics.cs] BLE capabilities and settings
requires: Ble
Inspects backend roles and readable/writable settings without starting the radio, then applies supported MTU and TX power preferences.

[scan_parameters.cs] Passive scan with duty-cycle settings
requires: Ble, Ble.Scan, Ble.ScanParameters
Sets a 25% scan duty cycle, watches passive duplicate reports for five seconds and stops cleanly. ArduinoBLE does not expose scan timing.

[manufacturer_data.cs] Manufacturer and service-data advertising
requires: Ble, Ble.Advertise
Builds a non-connectable advertisement with manufacturer bytes and battery service data. Replace the demo company ID with your assigned ID for production.

[device_info.cs] Device information and custom descriptors
requires: Ble, Ble.Server, Ble.Advertise
Standard manufacturer/model characteristics plus a readable, writable custom value with a user-description descriptor. Inspect and write from nRF Connect.

[gatt_explorer.cs] Central: discover services and descriptors
requires: Ble, Ble.Scan, Ble.Central, Ble.Read, BitConverter
Connects to the first heart-rate advertiser and lists services, characteristics and readable values. Change the service UUID to inspect your own peripheral.

[connection_options.cs] Central: connection timing and RSSI
requires: Ble, Ble.Scan, Ble.Central, Ble.Parameters, Ble.Rssi
Requests 30–50 ms connection intervals with a four-second supervision timeout and prints RSSI. Parameter changes are requests; the peer may reject them.

[bond_management.cs] Inspect stored BLE bonds
requires: Ble, Ble.Bonds
Lists bonded peer addresses without deleting anything. Set FORGET_ALL explicitly only when you intend to erase every saved pairing key.
